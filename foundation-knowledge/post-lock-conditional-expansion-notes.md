# Conditional Expansion Notes – Locked Inspection Baseline

## Purpose of This Document
This document records conditional next steps that would be taken if the scope or context of the Active Care Folder system changes in the future. It exists to demonstrate foresight and responsible planning without expanding scope prematurely.

The system remains locked as an inspection-ready baseline unless one of the conditions below is met.

## Condition 1: Operational Rollout Begins
If the system moves from assurance or limited use into active operational rollout across services, additional governance controls will be introduced.

These will include:
- staff onboarding and training safeguards
- clarity of roles and responsibilities during rollout
- change management and adoption controls
- monitoring for unintended use during early deployment

These controls will be documented as **Operational Rollout Controls** and reviewed before wider deployment.

Until rollout begins, these controls are intentionally out of scope.

## Condition 2: CQC Inspection Approaches
If a CQC inspection becomes imminent or is formally scheduled, an inspection preparation activity will be undertaken.

This will include:
- an inspector-style question and answer rehearsal
- validation that documentation remains accurate and current
- confirmation that staff-facing explanations are understood
- preparation of leadership and assurance representatives

This activity supports confidence and clarity during inspection but does not change system behaviour or access.

Until inspection is imminent, this activity is not required.

## Locked Baseline Confirmation
Unless one of the above conditions is met, the system and its governance documentation remain locked as an inspection-ready baseline.

Any future expansion is deliberate, documented, and versioned.
