# Using the Active Care Folder System Safely – Information for Staff

## Why This System Exists
The Active Care Folder system is designed to support safe, well-led care by helping teams organise and understand important information. It exists to support awareness, continuity, and good governance — not to replace professional judgement, training, or established care processes.

The system has been designed carefully to protect people using services, staff, and organisations.

## What the System Is For
The system is intended to:
- support awareness of care context
- help teams locate and manage information safely
- improve organisation and continuity
- support governance, oversight, and inspection readiness

It provides **context and structure**, not instructions.

## What the System Is NOT For
The system must not be used to:
- make clinical or care decisions
- replace professional judgement
- override care plans, risk assessments, or policies
- provide medical advice or instructions
- act as an emergency or crisis tool

Decisions about care must always follow professional standards and organisational processes.

## How the System Keeps People Safe
The system has been designed so that:
- access is controlled and proportionate
- sensitive information is introduced gradually
- information is presented without prompts or recommendations
- safeguards prevent accidental over-access
- expansion of access requires approval and review

This means the system supports staff without placing pressure on them to act on incomplete information.

## Your Responsibility as a User
As a user of the system, you are expected to:
- use the information as context, not instruction
- continue to follow training, policies, and care plans
- raise concerns if information appears unclear, outdated, or unsafe
- avoid making assumptions based on limited data

The system supports your work — it does not replace your professional responsibility.

## What to Do If You Are Unsure or Concerned
If you are unsure how to interpret information, or if something appears wrong:
- do not rely on the system alone
- follow normal escalation, safeguarding, or clinical pathways
- raise concerns through appropriate organisational channels

Using the right process protects you and the people you support.

## Learning and Improvement
Feedback from staff is important. The system is reviewed and improved over time based on:
- user feedback
- safety concerns
- learning from experience

Raising issues is encouraged and supported.

## A Final Reassurance
This system has been designed to support safe, responsible practice. It is not a test of performance, and it is not intended to place additional pressure on staff.

Your judgement, professionalism, and care remain central.
