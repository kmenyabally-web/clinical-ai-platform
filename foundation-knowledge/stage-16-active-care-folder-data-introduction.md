# Safe Introduction of Active Care Folder Data

## Purpose of Controlled Data Introduction
Introducing care-related information into a digital system carries inherent risk, particularly where data may influence decisions, understanding, or actions taken by staff. This document defines how Active Care Folder data would be introduced safely, gradually, and proportionately, ensuring that system capability does not outpace safeguards.

The purpose is to protect people using services, staff, and organisational accountability.

## Principle of Minimum Clinical Exposure
The system introduces care information using a minimum-exposure approach. Only information that provides context and orientation is considered first, while detailed, sensitive, or decision-critical data is deliberately excluded until further safeguards are proven.

This ensures the system supports understanding without influencing care decisions prematurely.

## First Category of Care Information Considered
The first category of Active Care Folder information considered for introduction is **descriptive, non-interpretive context**, such as:
- confirmation that an Active Care Folder exists
- high-level section titles without content
- non-clinical organisational references

This information does not describe health conditions, risks, behaviours, or interventions.

## Data Explicitly Excluded at Initial Introduction
The following types of data are explicitly excluded at this stage:
- diagnoses or medical conditions
- risk assessments or safeguarding information
- behavioural support plans
- medication records
- clinical notes or observations
- personal narratives or history

Exclusion prevents misinterpretation, inappropriate reliance, or harm.

## Protection Against Misuse or Over-Interpretation
The system is designed so that early-stage care information cannot be used to make clinical or support decisions. Information is presented as contextual only, with no prompts, recommendations, or decision-support features attached.

This ensures human judgement remains primary.

## Oversight and Review Before Further Expansion
Any move beyond this initial category requires renewed governance review, evidence of safe operation, and explicit approval. Progression is paused if risks are unclear or safeguards are incomplete.

## Alignment with CQC Expectations
This approach demonstrates proportionality, risk awareness, and respect for the sensitivity of care data. It aligns with CQC expectations for safe, well-led services that introduce digital systems cautiously and responsibly.
