# Readiness Definition for Expanding Beyond the First Controlled Data Read

## Purpose of Defining Expansion Readiness
In regulated health and social care systems, access to data must expand only when there is clear evidence that existing controls work reliably. Defining readiness for expanded data access ensures that growth in system capability does not outpace governance, safety, or assurance.

This document explains the conditions that must be met before any consideration is given to reading more data than the first controlled read.

## Principle of Earned Trust
Expanded data access is not assumed or automatic. Trust is earned through demonstrated system behaviour, verified enforcement, and documented oversight. Until these conditions are met, access remains intentionally limited.

## Stability of Existing Controls
Before expansion is considered, the system must show stable and predictable behaviour at the first controlled read level. This includes consistent enforcement of access boundaries, absence of unexpected access paths, and no unresolved security or governance concerns.

## Evidence of Ongoing Compliance
Evidence must exist that controls continue to operate as intended over time, not just during initial verification. This includes monitoring outcomes, review records, and confirmation that no unauthorised access attempts have succeeded.

## Role and Responsibility Clarity
Responsibilities for approving expanded access must be clearly defined and separated from system builders. Decisions to expand access must sit with appropriate governance, assurance, or information oversight roles.

## Risk Review Before Expansion
A structured review must be carried out to assess whether expanding access introduces new risks to people using services, staff, or regulatory compliance. Expansion is paused if risks are unclear, unmanaged, or insufficiently mitigated.

## Formal Approval Requirement
No expansion beyond the first controlled read may occur without explicit, documented approval. Approval confirms that evidence has been reviewed, risks are understood, and expansion aligns with regulatory expectations.

## Alignment with CQC Expectations
This readiness approach demonstrates proactive risk management, controlled system evolution, and inspection readiness. It shows that access expansion is deliberate, governed, and evidence-led, in line with CQC expectations for safe and well-led digital systems.
