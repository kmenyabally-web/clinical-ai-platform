# CQC Inspector Walkthrough Narrative – Active Care Folder System

## Purpose of This Narrative
This document provides a clear, end-to-end walkthrough of how the Active Care Folder system operates from a regulatory and safety perspective. It is written to support inspection by explaining how risk is controlled at each stage of access, data introduction, and system use.

The narrative reflects deliberate design choices made to prioritise safety, accountability, and proportionality.

## Initial Access and Identity Control
An inspector entering the system would first observe that access is tightly controlled through authenticated identity. Authentication confirms who the user is, but does not grant access to care data by default.

Roles are assigned deliberately and separately from system capability, ensuring that identity alone never equates to permission.

## Early System Use Without Care Data
At initial sign-in, users are presented only with contextual information confirming system status and their role. No care information is visible at this stage.

This demonstrates that the system can operate safely without touching sensitive data and that access is not assumed.

## Controlled Introduction of Data
Care information is introduced only after governance gates are passed. The first category of information is intentionally limited to non-clinical, descriptive context. This allows orientation without influencing care decisions.

Sensitive information such as diagnoses, risks, behaviour plans, or medication remains excluded until further safeguards are proven.

## Protection Against Misinterpretation
Where care-related context is shown, the system avoids language or design cues that imply authority or instruction. Users are supported to understand what information means — and what it does not mean.

Decision-making remains with trained professionals and established care processes.

## Enforcement and Verification
Access controls are enforced at multiple layers, ensuring that users cannot read beyond what has been approved. Enforcement is verified before trust is extended, and failure to verify results in halted progression.

This provides confidence that safeguards work in practice, not just on paper.

## Governance of Expansion
Any expansion of access requires formal approval, independent review, and documented evidence. Expansion cannot occur informally, temporarily, or through urgency.

Drift is actively monitored and corrected to prevent gradual erosion of controls.

## Review, Feedback, and Learning
The system supports review and correction of information over time. Users can raise concerns safely, corrections are handled through controlled processes, and learning feeds back into governance.

This supports continuous improvement without blame.

## Overall Inspection Assurance
From an inspection perspective, the system demonstrates:
- controlled and proportionate access
- clear separation between capability and permission
- protection against misuse and misinterpretation
- deliberate, evidence-led expansion
- alignment with CQC expectations for safe and well-led digital systems

This walkthrough shows that safety and governance are integral to the system’s design and operation.
