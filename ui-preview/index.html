How Readiness Levels Are Determined

Readiness levels are set by organisational leadership. They reflect governance confidence, not evidence storage. Levels are reviewed periodically and are not updated in real time. The platform does not calculate scores automatically.

Readiness levels:

  • Not started: No defined framework or assurance yet
  • In progress: Framework defined but assurance incomplete
  • Defined: Governance and processes documented and understood
  • Reviewed: Internal review completed with no major gaps identified
  • Assured: Leadership confident and inspection-ready

Readiness levels support inspection preparation and self-assurance. They do not replace professional judgement or operational systems.

Care Folder Capability Status

Care folders exist as a future capability but are intentionally disabled. This is a governance-first, inspection-safe decision.

Status: Not enabled

Mode: Governance-first

Reason: Care folders are intentionally disabled until organisational readiness, access controls, and assurance requirements are formally met.

Requirements before care folders can be enabled:

  • Named accountable lead for care governance
  • Access control and role model approved
  • Data protection and safeguarding readiness confirmed
  • CQC readiness baseline achieved
  • Formal approval recorded by senior leadership

This platform does not enable care records until governance is proven. This protects people, staff, and the organisation.

How Care Folders Are Introduced (Governed Transition)

Care folders are not a default feature of the platform. They are introduced only after inspection readiness is demonstrated. Introduction is a formal organisational decision, not a technical switch. Governance, access controls, and safeguards are reviewed first. Enablement is scoped, monitored, and can be paused or reversed.

Transition steps:

  1. Inspection readiness baseline achieved
  2. Governance and accountability confirmed
  3. Access control and role boundaries approved
  4. Data protection and safeguarding readiness verified
  5. Formal leadership approval recorded
  6. Care folder capability enabled in a controlled manner

Care folders are introduced only when it is safe and appropriate to do so. This staged approach protects people, staff, and organisations.
